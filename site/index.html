What is RemoteFM1

Players for FM-1 firmwares, everywhere people make music.

RemoteFM1 is a family of apps that run firmwares written for the M-VAVE FM-1 synth, on the screen in front of you. The panel, the screen and the sound behave as on the real unit. If someone owns an FM-1, it can even act as the keyboard.

Web browser

remotefm1.com. Nothing to install: pick a firmware and play.

iPhone and iPad

The RemoteFM1 app, and as an AUv3 instrument inside music apps.

Android

The RemoteFM1 app for phones and tablets.

Ableton Move

FM-1 firmwares on the Move itself, played with its pads and knobs.

What is an RFM1 build

One file. Every player.

An .rfm1 is your firmware, built for players instead of for the FM-1's chip. It is made from the same source code, so it sounds the same. One file works in every RemoteFM1 player, on any computer or phone. It never changes anyone's FM-1.

Why make one

More people play your firmware.

Try before flashing

Anyone can hear and play your firmware in a browser in seconds, even without an FM-1. Flashing is a big step; trying is not.

In the studio

On iPad your firmware becomes an instrument inside music apps, with its own sound and screen.

On new hardware

On an Ableton Move your firmware plays from the Move's pads and knobs, and can even take over its screen.

Your updates reach people

Once you are listed, every new release with an .rfm1 attached is picked up automatically, overnight.

Your credit stays on it

Your name, licence and a link to the exact source are inside the file, and the players show them.

Your FM-1 build is untouched

The .rfm1 is a separate build. The firmware people flash on their FM-1 stays exactly the same, byte for byte.

For firmware authors

Add RemoteFM1 to your releases.

Ship two extra files with each release: the .rfm1 for every RemoteFM1 player, and the .rfm1m for the Ableton Move. Your FM-1 firmware itself doesn't change.

Build the .rfm1

Run one build command. Firmwares built on fm1-base have it built in; others use the FM1VST build tools, which already know many firmwares.

Check it

Two free tools check the file and play it: it must start, make sound and draw its screen.

Build the Move version

One Docker command turns the .rfm1 into an .rfm1m and checks that it sounds exactly the same. About half a minute.

Release

Attach both files to your GitHub release. A ready-made workflow can build and attach them at every release.

Using Claude Code? The add-remotefm1 skill does all four steps inside your firmware's repository and asks you before adding any extras:

/plugin marketplace add Quixotic7/remotefm1format
/plugin install remotefm1-format@remotefm1

Then ask for your firmware to be listed: a link to your release is all we need.

Do I need to change my code?

Usually not. Many firmwares build as they are. The optional extras below are small additions you can make later, one at a time.

Will it change the firmware people flash on their FM-1?

No. The .rfm1 is a separate build. Your FM-1 firmware stays exactly as it is.

What do I need to build the Move version?

Docker on a Mac with Apple silicon or an arm64 Linux machine (other computers work, slowly). The release workflow can do it on GitHub's own machines instead. The Move document has the commands.

Every player

The full FM-1

Keys, knobs, buttons, the screen and the sound, exactly as your code draws and plays them. Saved settings and patterns are kept.

Your skin and panel labels on the on-screen FM-1, and your web editor connected to the firmware as if it were a real unit.

Optional extras

Make it feel at home. Only if you want to.

A plain .rfm1 already works everywhere. These extras are small additions, each one optional. Players that don't know an extra simply ignore it.

Browser · iOS · Android

Skin

Your own background picture and colours on the on-screen FM-1. Players can turn skins off.

Browser · Android · Move

Panel labels

Name the buttons and knobs for what they do in your firmware. They can change with each page.

Move

Readouts

While a knob turns, the Move shows what it controls and its real value, and can say it out loud. Often this needs no code at all.

Move

Move takeover

Your own Move screen, pad colours and controls, for a firmware that wants to feel made for the Move. A bigger project.

Move · early

Engine parameters

A page of your sound engine's settings on the Move's eight knobs. New, and still growing.

Browser · iOS · Android · Move

Audio input

Live sound from a microphone or line in goes into your firmware: for samplers, loopers and effects.

Fantasy CFW

Player-only firmware

A "Fantasy CFW" exists only in RemoteFM1: much more memory, and a shared folder for samples and projects.

Move

Spoken words

With the Move's screen reader on, your firmware can say what its screen shows, so blind players can use it too.

Move

Second core

A firmware that uses both of the FM-1's cores can use two on the Move too, so heavier sounds fit.

The full technical details of each extra are in the capabilities document.

For authors and fans

Ask for a firmware to be listed.

The RemoteFM1 catalog is the list players download firmwares from. Anyone can ask for a firmware to be added: its author, or a fan who wants to play it.

  • Its releases have .rfm1 files: a link is all we need. The firmware is listed exactly as its author made it, and each newer release is picked up overnight.
  • It only has FM-1 releases (.fwsc): we may build the .rfm1 ourselves. Because we then make and share a copy of someone else's work, its source code must be public on GitHub with a licence that lets anyone share it (GPL, MPL, MIT…), it needs a normal release like v1.2.3, and everything in it must be shareable: no copied preset dumps. The form asks about these.

What happens next

We try the firmware in RemoteFM1 and add it to the catalog. If we're not the author, we tell the author. The Move version follows at the next Move catalog update.

Fill in the form.

It asks for a link to the firmware's release, a short description and a picture. The licence questions are only for firmwares we build ourselves.

Ask to list a firmware

Read the catalog policy for the details: updates, removals.

What does "hosted only" mean?

For firmwares we build ourselves: a few contain material whose rights allow sharing it for download but not shipping it inside an app. Those are offered for download only. We decide this with the author.

How do I remove my firmware, or change its description?

Open the change or remove form. You can also keep your card text and picture in a file in your repository (RemoteFM1Metadata.json).
